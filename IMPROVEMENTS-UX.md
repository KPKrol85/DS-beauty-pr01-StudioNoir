# Studio Noir — UX Improvements

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-05
**Project type:** Static multi-page website (Vite MPA build; vanilla HTML, CSS custom properties, and ES modules; service worker with a build-generated precache) — Polish-language demonstration site for a hair studio, KP_Code Digital Studio
**Analysis mode:** Evidence-based UX improvement review
**Focus:** Project-wide UX

## Improvement overview

The home page is a long one-page layout — 9.8 viewport heights at 1280×800 and 17.9 at 375×812 — with anchor navigation, a three-step booking widget backed by direct-contact options, a gallery lightbox, and a quick-contact bar below 768px. Three long legal documents share the same header. The interaction foundations are solid: the mobile menu and the lightbox manage focus as modal dialogs, the booking summary is a live region, and booking choices use dedicated controls with a non-color selected state.

The main opportunities lie in the connections between these parts rather than in the parts themselves. The studio's address and opening hours cannot be reached from the navigation. A service or stylist chosen while browsing has to be chosen again in the booking widget, which also omits two listed services. The widget does not say which choice is missing before confirmation. The current-section indicator misreports the position outside the linked sections. The lightbox shows one image at a time, without a caption. Confirmed defects found during the analysis are listed under the analysis limitations and are not presented as improvements.

## Proposed improvements

### IMP-UX-01 — Add the location and contact section to the main navigation

- **Status:** Completed — added "Kontakt" as a seventh main-navigation entry on the home page (`#location`) and the three legal pages (`/index.html#location`), keeping the existing "Rezerwacja" booking link; tighter desktop navigation spacing keeps the header at its previous height. Desktop navigation was verified at 900, 1024, and 1280px and the mobile menu at 375×667; `npm run test:nav` and `npm run build` passed.
- **Affected journey:** Finding the studio's address, opening hours, phone number, e-mail, and map link from the header navigation — inline on desktop and in the mobile menu — on the home page and the three legal pages.
- **Evidence:** `index.html:57-64` (six navigation links, none to `#location`); `index.html:386-414` (the only place that shows the address and opening hours); `index.html:443-461` (footer without address, hours, or a link to the section); `privacy.html:54-59`, `terms.html:54-59`, `cookies.html:43-48` (the same six links on the legal pages); `README.md:229` (header duplicated in four files); `tests/nav.spec.js:9` (expected link list). Chromium probe: no link on the home page targets `#location`; the section starts 15.8 viewport heights down at 375×812 and 8.4 at 1280×800.
- **Current experience:** The navigation covers About through Booking. The Lokalizacja section — address, phone, e-mail, "Otwórz mapę", and opening hours — is the second-to-last section of the home page, and nothing links to it, so visitors reach it only by scrolling past the testimonials. A legal page offers no direct path to it at all. The quick-contact bar below 768px offers booking, calling, and Instagram, but not the address or hours.
- **Proposed improvement:** Add a navigation entry for the location section (for example "Kontakt" or "Lokalizacja") to the shared header navigation, pointing to `#location` on the home page and to `/index.html#location` on the legal pages.
- **Expected user value:** Visitors who want to know where the studio is or when it is open — typical questions before booking or visiting — reach the answer in one step from any content page, including on mobile.
- **Implementation scope:** The navigation list in the four duplicated headers and the expected link list in `tests/nav.spec.js`. `js/nav.js` and `js/header.js` need no structural change: a new `.nav__link` already closes the mobile menu on activation and is tracked by the section indicator. Desktop width is tight — in the probe environment the header had no spare width at 900 and 1024px (see limitations) — so the change either fits a seventh entry or replaces the "Rezerwacja" link, whose `#booking` target duplicates the adjacent "Umów wizytę" button. That choice is an owner decision and also changes the tracked set in IMP-UX-04. Preserve the 900px breakpoint, the modal mobile menu, the call to action, and the theme toggle. Adding address or hours to the footer is outside this scope.
- **Acceptance criteria:** On the home page and all three legal pages, the main navigation contains an entry that leads to the location section, both inline on desktop and in the mobile menu. Activating it in the mobile menu closes the menu and shows the location section. At 900, 1024, and 1280px the desktop header is no taller than before the change and causes no horizontal overflow. At 375×667 the last control of the open mobile menu still ends above the quick-contact bar. `npm run test:nav` passes with the updated link list.
- **Impact:** High
- **Effort:** Small

### IMP-UX-02 — Start a booking from the service and stylist cards with the choice preselected

- **Status:** Completed — every service and stylist card now has a "Zarezerwuj" link to `#booking`, and the booking widget offers all five listed services. With JavaScript the link selects the matching option through the existing selection function (`aria-pressed`, selected state, summary), keeps the other choice, and focuses the option after the anchor navigation; where the anchor position would leave the option below the visible area or behind the quick-contact bar, the same scroll continues just far enough to show it. Without JavaScript the link stays a plain link to `#booking`. `npm run build` passed; on the `npm run dev` page all eight links were verified by pointer and keyboard in Chromium and Firefox, with combined preselection enabling confirmation, replacement in the widget, Tab order, disabled JavaScript, and viewports from 360×640 and 667×375 to 1366×768, where every viewport that already showed the option kept the plain anchor position. WebKit was not tested.
- **Affected journey:** Moving from the Services and Stylists sections to the booking widget.
- **Evidence:** `index.html:136-162` (five service cards without an action); `index.html:198-238` (three stylist cards without an action; `index.html:221` names men's hairdressing as Leon Zawadzki's speciality); `index.html:282-295` (booking options: three services, three stylists); `js/booking.js:28-52` (an option is selected only by activating it in the widget). Chromium probe: the booking service options are Strzyżenie, Koloryzacja, and Stylizacja; the service cards also list Fryzjer męski and Pielęgnacja.
- **Current experience:** Services and stylists are presented as static cards. A visitor who decides on "Koloryzacja" or on a particular stylist must find the booking section through the navigation or a call to action and select the same service and stylist again in the widget. Two of the five listed services — Fryzjer męski and Pielęgnacja — have no option in the widget, although men's hairdressing is the speciality named on Leon Zawadzki's card.
- **Proposed improvement:** Give each service card and stylist card a booking action that brings the booking section into view with the matching option already selected, and make the widget's service options cover every listed service.
- **Expected user value:** A decision made while browsing carries straight into booking — one action instead of navigating and selecting again — and no listed service is missing from the booking choices.
- **Implementation scope:** Card markup and booking options in `index.html`, preselection in `js/booking.js` through the existing selection function, and placement of the action with the existing `.button` variants. Without JavaScript the actions are ordinary links to `#booking`, where the existing `<noscript>` fallback applies. Preserve the `data-booking-*` hooks, the `aria-pressed` synchronization, the summary live region, the rule that confirmation needs both choices, and the widget's demonstration-only behavior. No date selection, contact fields, or package booking: the owner confirms the canonical option list, and the pricing packages stay out unless decided otherwise. The option-coverage change can ship first on its own.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Every service card and stylist card offers a booking action. Activating it by pointer or keyboard shows the booking section with the matching option selected (`aria-pressed="true"`, summary updated), and the next Tab press continues inside the booking widget. Preselecting a service from one card and a stylist from another enables confirmation. Every service listed in the Services section has a matching booking option. Choosing another option in the widget replaces the preselected one. With JavaScript disabled, the card actions still lead to `#booking`.
- **Impact:** High
- **Effort:** Medium

### IMP-UX-03 — Tell users which booking choice is still missing

- **Status:** Completed — one booking state now drives the missing-service/stylist hint beside confirmation and the existing summary status. The unavailable button stays in the Tab sequence with `aria-disabled` and an `aria-describedby` hint; confirmation still requires both choices. Chromium and Firefox checks on `npm run dev` passed for all four states, keyboard/pointer guards, ready confirmation, and IMP-UX-02 card preselection, focus recovery, and no-JavaScript links. At 375×667px the hint and button are visible together in both themes without horizontal overflow. `npm run build` and `git diff --check` passed; post-confirmation defects remain outside scope.
- **Affected journey:** The booking widget before confirmation (steps 1–3).
- **Evidence:** `js/booking.js:15-26` (one generic status until both choices exist; native `disabled`); `index.html:297-317` (the confirmation button in step 3, explained only by the separate summary); `css/sections.css:136-144` and `css/sections.css:322-328` (the summary follows the steps below 900px); `README.md:191`. Chromium probe: after choosing only a service, the status still reads "Wybierz opcje"; Tab from the last stylist option moves to the "Zadzwoń" fallback link, skipping the disabled button; at 375×812 the status sits 423px below the confirmation button; the button has no accessible description.
- **Current experience:** The confirmation button stays disabled until both a service and a stylist are chosen. The only explanation is the summary status "Wybierz opcje" ("Choose options"), which does not say which choice is missing and does not change after the first choice. Below 900px the summary comes after all three steps, so the disabled button appears without a visible reason nearby. Keyboard users tab past the disabled button, and assistive technology receives no reason for its state.
- **Proposed improvement:** State the missing choice — service, stylist, or both — next to the confirmation button and in the summary status, and expose that hint as the button's accessible description while confirmation is unavailable.
- **Expected user value:** Users see why they cannot confirm yet and what to do next, at the point where they try, on every viewport and with any input method.
- **Implementation scope:** `updateSummary` in `js/booking.js`, the step 3 markup in `index.html`, and minimal token-based styling. Whether the unavailable button stays focusable (for example through `aria-disabled`) is an implementation decision; confirmation must remain impossible until both choices exist. Preserve the selection logic, `aria-pressed`, and the live region. The confirmation outcome and the post-confirmation state contain separate defects (see limitations) and are out of scope; they also touch `updateSummary`, so coordinate the order of work.
- **Acceptance criteria:** With nothing selected, the hint names both choices; after selecting only a service it names the stylist, and vice versa. Once both are selected, the hint gives way to the ready state and the button becomes available. At 375px the hint is visible in step 3 together with the button. While confirmation is unavailable, the button's accessible description matches the current hint. Confirmation still cannot be triggered while a choice is missing.
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Effort:** Small

### IMP-UX-04 — Keep the current-section indicator accurate across the whole page

- **Affected journey:** Orientation through the header navigation on the home page, inline on desktop and in the mobile menu.
- **Evidence:** `js/header.js:10` (`aria-current="page"`); `js/header.js:35` and `js/header.js:53` (the first tracked section is marked at start); `js/header.js:46` (the scroll fallback defaults to the first section); `js/header.js:79-80` (the previous link stays marked when no tracked section is in the detection zone); `css/components.css:356-368`; `index.html:346-424` (testimonials, location, and closing call to action have no navigation entry); `css/base.css:171-177`. Chromium probe at 1280×800, described below.
- **Current experience:** On load, "O nas" is marked current while the hero fills 626px of the 800px viewport. From the testimonials section to the end of the page, "Rezerwacja" stays marked. An instant scroll back to the top kept "Rezerwacja" marked over the hero; anchor scrolling is instant whenever reduced motion disables smooth scrolling, for example on the logo's `#top` link. The state is exposed as `aria-current="page"`, which denotes the current page rather than a position within it.
- **Proposed improvement:** Mark a navigation link only while its own section is the current reading position, mark none where no entry applies (the hero and sections without a link), and expose the state with an `aria-current` value meant for a location within the page.
- **Expected user value:** The highlighted link and the announced current state become a reliable answer to "where am I?" instead of pointing to a section the visitor has not reached yet or has already left.
- **Implementation scope:** The section spy in `js/header.js`, in both the `IntersectionObserver` path and the scroll fallback; the active-link styles stay as they are. Preserve link-driven tracking, the header-offset logic, the observer thresholds unless re-tuning is required, and the mini call to action. Implement after IMP-UX-01, which changes the set of tracked sections.
- **Acceptance criteria:** At scroll position 0 after load, no navigation link carries `is-active` or `aria-current`. Loading `/index.html#services` marks "Usługi". Each linked section still marks its own link while it is in reading position. With the testimonials section in reading position, no link is marked unless that section has its own entry. After an instant scroll to the top, no link remains marked. The marked link uses `aria-current="location"` (or `"true"`), never `"page"`. The scroll fallback without `IntersectionObserver` behaves the same.
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Effort:** Small

### IMP-UX-05 — Browse the gallery inside the lightbox

- **Affected journey:** Gallery and lightbox preview on the home page.
- **Evidence:** `js/lightbox.js:34-45` (opening shows one image); `js/lightbox.js:58-63` (each thumbnail opens independently); `js/lightbox.js:71-98` (only Escape and Tab are handled); `index.html:248-267` (six thumbnails with descriptive `alt` text and no visible captions); `index.html:463-471` (the dialog holds a close button and the image, labelled "Podgląd zdjęcia"); `assets/img/gallery-1.svg:5` (placeholder text "Look 1"). Chromium probe: the open dialog's only visible text is "✕", it has one focusable control, and ArrowRight does not change the image.
- **Current experience:** Each thumbnail opens its own preview. To see the next image, the visitor closes the dialog — focus returns to the thumbnail — and opens the next one, six times for the whole gallery. The dialog shows no caption or position: descriptions such as "Koloryzacja ombre" exist only as `alt` text, so sighted visitors see the image (currently a "Look 1"–"Look 6" placeholder) without learning which look or service it shows.
- **Proposed improvement:** Let visitors move to the previous and next gallery image from within the open lightbox, with buttons and the Left and Right arrow keys, and show the current image's description and position (for example "Koloryzacja ombre · 2 / 6").
- **Expected user value:** The portfolio can be browsed in one continuous view, every visitor gets the same description of what each image shows, and the position shows how much of the gallery remains.
- **Implementation scope:** `js/lightbox.js`, the lightbox markup in `index.html`, and the lightbox rules in `css/components.css`. Derive the sequence from the existing `[data-lightbox]` thumbnails and the caption from each thumbnail's `alt`, so the gallery markup remains the single source. Preserve the dialog semantics, the focus trap, closing by Escape, overlay, or close button, the scroll lock, and focus return — to the thumbnail of the image shown last. Depends on the separate lightbox height defect (see limitations): the added controls and caption make the dialog taller, so it must first fit short viewports.
- **Acceptance criteria:** In the open lightbox, the next and previous buttons and the Right and Left arrow keys move through all six images, with consistent and visible behavior at the first and last image. After each change, the image's `alt`, the visible caption, and the position describe the image shown, and the change is conveyed to assistive technology. Tab cycles only through the dialog's controls. Escape, the overlay, and the close button close the dialog and return focus to the thumbnail of the image shown last. At 375×667 and 1440×900 the image, caption, and controls fit within the viewport.
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Effort:** Medium

## Selection summary

- **Relevance:** The five proposals cover the visitor's main tasks — finding visit details (01), turning a browsing decision into a booking (02), completing the booking choices (03), knowing where one is on the long one-page layout (04), and browsing the portfolio (05). Each improves an existing journey with existing content and adds no new product scope. None repeats completed work from the archived UI, Quality, or Workflow cycles; there is no active plan or UI report.
- **Dependencies:** 04 follows 01, because 01 changes which sections have a navigation entry, especially if "Rezerwacja" is replaced. 02 and 03 both change `js/booking.js` and the booking markup, so they should be implemented one after the other. 05 depends on fixing the lightbox height defect first. The booking-confirmation defects listed under the limitations share `updateSummary` with 03; fixing them before 02 and 03 avoids building new paths into misleading confirmation feedback.
- **Independent work:** 01, the 02/03 pair, and 05 can be implemented independently of one another.
- **Scope:** Three Small proposals (01, 03, 04) and two Medium ones (02, 05), each verifiable with a focused browser check, plus `npm run test:nav` for the navigation markup change in 01. Together they form a practical candidate backlog for a focused development day, with no guarantee that all five fit into one day.
- **Considered but not selected:** Instagram link behavior — on a desktop viewport the footer "Instagram" link, marked `target="_blank"` and pointing to the profile, navigated the same tab to the `ig.me` messaging URL after 900ms (`js/mobile-cta.js:5-38`). This is the documented behavior of `data-instagram-link` links (`README.md:25`); it reaches fewer visitors than the selected journeys, deciding which destination each Instagram link should open is a product choice, and the adjacent "Napisz DM" placeholder defect should be fixed first. In-page tables of contents for the legal documents — 14, 18, and 9 numbered sections, 14.1 to 24.1 viewport heights at 375px, no in-page links — would help a secondary journey with lower impact than the selected ones.

## Analysis limitations

- Runtime behavior was inspected in a single probe run of headless Chromium, driven by the environment's global Playwright, against the canonical sources served by a plain static server on localhost, with all external requests blocked. Without Vite, `import.meta.env` is undefined, so `js/main.js` threw a `TypeError` after all initializers had run. The service worker, the production build, and `npm run dev` were not exercised. Dependencies are not installed, so no build, test, or preview command was run.
- Text renders largely in fallback fonts, because the bundled WOFF2 files cover only a Latin Extended subset (recorded in the archived UI report). Width-dependent measurements may therefore differ in other environments. In this environment the desktop header measured 136px at 900px and 88px at 1024px, against 69px at 1280px, with no spare width between the logo and the navigation; this informs the scope of IMP-UX-01 but is not reported as a finding.
- Real mobile devices, touch input, the Instagram app hand-off, Safari, and Firefox were not tested. Impact ratings are relative judgments based on the implementation, not on usability research or analytics.
- Confirmed defects observed during the analysis are kept outside this report and belong to audit or defect work:
  - The booking widget collects no contact details and, according to `README.md:13`, sends and stores nothing, yet it promises a call back within 15 minutes and SMS/DM confirmation. After confirmation it reports "Rezerwacja wstępnie zapisana" ("booking provisionally saved") and relabels the button "Wysłano" ("Sent") (`index.html:303-308`, `js/booking.js:54-59`).
  - After confirmation, changing a choice re-enables the button while it still reads "Wysłano", and the status returns to "Gotowe do potwierdzenia" (`js/booking.js:15-26`, `js/booking.js:54-59`).
  - In the mobile menu, the "Umów wizytę" button is not a `.nav__link`, so activating it scrolls to `#booking` behind the full-screen menu, which stays open with `<main>` inert (`js/nav.js:73-77`, `index.html:65-66`, `css/components.css:323-342`).
  - The fixed quick-contact bar (`z-index: 180`, outside `<main>`) renders above the open modal menu, which sits in the header's `z-index: 100` stacking context, and stays operable: tapping its "Umów wizytę" while the menu is open navigates behind the menu (`css/components.css:259`, `css/components.css:420-435`, `index.html:427-441`).
  - The lightbox has no height limit: at 1366×768 and 1280×720 the image extends beyond the viewport and the close button sits entirely above it (`css/components.css:493-530`).
  - The footer "Napisz DM" link uses a literal placeholder URL, `https://www.instagram.com/direct/t/[username]`, on all four content pages (`index.html:450`, `privacy.html:359`, `terms.html:449`, `cookies.html:325`).
  - "Otwórz mapę" opens `https://maps.google.com` without the studio's address, and the Facebook link opens `https://www.facebook.com` (`index.html:400`, `index.html:452`).
